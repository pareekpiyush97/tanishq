Atelier

Five stages, a karigar at each one.

  1. 01

    Wax

    The form is carved in wax at true size, so the ring fits before any gold is poured.

  2. 02

    Casting

    Molten 22K gold fills the mould. The wax burns away and leaves the shape behind.

  3. 03

    Setting

    Each stone is seated under a 10× loupe and the prongs are folded over by hand.

  4. 04

    Polishing

    Seven grades of polish, finishing with jeweller's rouge on a cotton wheel.

  5. 05

    Hallmark

    A BIS assay centre tests the gold and laser-marks the purity grade and a 6-digit HUID inside the band.
